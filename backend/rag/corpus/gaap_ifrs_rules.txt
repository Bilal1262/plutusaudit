STANDARD: IFRS 15
PARAGRAPH: 31
TOPIC: Software Subscriptions and SaaS
RULE: A software subscription or SaaS license is a service delivered over time. It represents a performance obligation satisfied ratably over the subscription period. The full invoice amount must NOT be expensed immediately. Initial recognition: Debit Prepaid Expenses (Asset), Credit Accounts Payable. Monthly amortization: Debit Subscription Expense (OPEX), Credit Prepaid Expenses. Example: A 12-month software license for $2,400 creates $200/month expense.
KEYWORDS: software, subscription, saas, license, annual, monthly, cloud, platform

STANDARD: ASC 720
PARAGRAPH: 25-1
TOPIC: Office Supplies and Consumables
RULE: Office supplies purchased for immediate operational use are expensed as incurred. They do not meet capitalization criteria as they have no future economic benefit beyond the current period. Journal entry: Debit Office Supplies Expense (OPEX), Credit Accounts Payable. No deferral required. Examples: paper, pens, printer ink, folders, stationery.
KEYWORDS: office supplies, stationery, paper, consumables, pens, printer, ink, folders

STANDARD: IAS 16
PARAGRAPH: 7
TOPIC: Property Plant and Equipment - Capitalization
RULE: An item must be capitalized (treated as CAPEX) when it is probable that future economic benefits will flow to the entity AND the cost can be measured reliably AND the useful life exceeds one accounting period (typically 1 year). Computers, laptops, servers, and equipment with useful life over 1 year must be capitalized. Journal entry: Debit Computer Equipment / Fixed Assets (CAPEX), Credit Accounts Payable. Depreciation is then recorded over the useful life.
KEYWORDS: laptop, computer, server, equipment, hardware, machinery, furniture, vehicle, asset

---
STANDARD: IAS 16
PARAGRAPH: 10
TOPIC: Low-Value Assets and Materiality Threshold
RULE: Items below the company's capitalization threshold (commonly $500-$2,500 depending on policy) may be expensed immediately regardless of useful life. Keyboards, mice, small peripherals, and accessories typically fall below this threshold. Journal entry: Debit IT Equipment Expense (OPEX), Credit Accounts Payable.
KEYWORDS: keyboard, mouse, peripheral, accessory, cable, monitor, headset, webcam

---
STANDARD: ASC 720
PARAGRAPH: 25-1
TOPIC: Consulting and Professional Services - Expense Classification
RULE: Consulting services, professional fees, and advisory fees received and paid by the entity are operating expenses recognized when services are rendered. Journal entry: Debit Professional Services Expense (OPEX), Credit Accounts Payable. These are NEVER classified under ASC 606 or IFRS 15 - those standards govern revenue recognition for the party delivering services, not the party receiving and paying for them.
KEYWORDS: consulting, advisory, professional services, strategy, management consulting, advisory fee, professional fees

STANDARD: IAS 17 / IFRS 16
PARAGRAPH: 22
TOPIC: Office Rent and Lease Payments
RULE: Short-term lease payments (lease term 12 months or less) or low-value asset leases are expensed as incurred under IFRS 16 simplified approach. Journal entry: Debit Rent Expense (OPEX), Credit Accounts Payable. Long-term leases (over 12 months) require right-of-use asset recognition: Debit Right-of-Use Asset, Credit Lease Liability.
KEYWORDS: rent, lease, office space, premises, facility, building, coworking, workspace

STANDARD: ASC 946 / IAS 38
PARAGRAPH: 18
TOPIC: Marketing and Advertising Expenses
RULE: Marketing, advertising, and promotional expenses are expensed as incurred. They do not meet the criteria for intangible asset recognition as future benefits are uncertain and cannot be reliably measured. Journal entry: Debit Marketing Expense / Advertising Expense (OPEX), Credit Accounts Payable.
KEYWORDS: marketing, advertising, promotion, campaign, social media, digital marketing, branding, PR

---
STANDARD: ASC 740
PARAGRAPH: 10
TOPIC: Travel and Entertainment Expenses
RULE: Travel expenses for business purposes are expensed as incurred in the period the travel occurs. Journal entry: Debit Travel Expense (OPEX), Credit Accounts Payable or Employee Expense Payable. Entertainment expenses require documentation of business purpose. Meals are typically 50% deductible for tax purposes but fully expensed for accounting purposes.
KEYWORDS: travel, flight, hotel, accommodation, meals, entertainment, taxi, transport, conference

---
STANDARD: IAS 38
PARAGRAPH: 54
TOPIC: Research and Development
RULE: Research costs must always be expensed as incurred - they cannot be capitalized. Development costs may be capitalized only when technical feasibility, intention to complete, ability to use or sell, and reliable measurement are all demonstrated. Journal entry for research: Debit R&D Expense (OPEX), Credit Accounts Payable.
KEYWORDS: research, development, R&D, prototype, feasibility study, innovation, testing

STANDARD: ASC 420
PARAGRAPH: 15
TOPIC: Utilities and Operating Expenses
RULE: Utility expenses including electricity, water, internet, and telecommunications are operating expenses incurred in the period of use. Journal entry: Debit Utilities Expense (OPEX), Credit Accounts Payable. These are never capitalized.
KEYWORDS: electricity, utilities, internet, broadband, telephone, water, gas, telecoms, wifi

---
STANDARD: IAS 19
PARAGRAPH: 11
TOPIC: Employee Training and Development
RULE: Employee training, development, and education costs are expensed as incurred. They cannot be capitalized as intangible assets because the company does not control the future economic benefits (employee could leave). Journal entry: Debit Training Expense (OPEX), Credit Accounts Payable.
KEYWORDS: training, education, course, certification, workshop, seminar, conference registration, learning

STANDARD: ASC 350
PARAGRAPH: 40
TOPIC: Software Development Costs - Internal Use
RULE: Internal-use software development costs during the application development stage are capitalized. Preliminary project stage and post-implementation stage costs are expensed. For purchased software with useful life over 1 year: capitalize and amortize. Journal entry: Debit Intangible Assets - Software (CAPEX), Credit Accounts Payable.
KEYWORDS: software development, custom software, implementation, system integration, ERP, CRM, development hours

STANDARD: IFRS 15
PARAGRAPH: 106
TOPIC: Maintenance and Support Contracts
RULE: Annual maintenance and support contracts for software or equipment are recognized ratably over the contract period, similar to subscriptions. If the maintenance period spans multiple accounting periods, the prepaid portion is deferred. Journal entry: Debit Prepaid Maintenance, Credit Accounts Payable. Monthly: Debit Maintenance Expense, Credit Prepaid Maintenance.
KEYWORDS: maintenance, support, annual support, warranty, service contract, helpdesk, technical support

---
STANDARD: IAS 2
PARAGRAPH: 10
TOPIC: Inventory and Stock Purchases
RULE: Goods purchased for resale or for use in production are recorded as inventory (asset) not as expense. Journal entry: Debit Inventory (Asset), Credit Accounts Payable. The inventory is expensed as Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) when the goods are sold. Raw materials for manufacturing follow the same treatment.
KEYWORDS: inventory, stock, goods, raw materials, components, parts, resale, manufacturing, merchandise

STANDARD: ASC 835
PARAGRAPH: 20
TOPIC: Interest and Finance Charges
RULE: Interest expense on borrowings and finance charges are expensed in the period incurred. They are not capitalized unless directly attributable to the acquisition of a qualifying asset under IAS 23. Journal entry: Debit Interest Expense (Finance Cost), Credit Accounts Payable or Accrued Interest.
KEYWORDS: interest, finance charge, bank fee, loan fee, borrowing cost, credit facility

---
STANDARD: IFRS 15
PARAGRAPH: 38
TOPIC: Recruitment and Staffing Agency Fees
RULE: Recruitment fees paid to staffing agencies for hiring permanent employees are expensed as incurred as they represent the cost of the recruitment service already rendered. Journal entry: Debit Recruitment Expense (OPEX), Credit Accounts Payable. They are not capitalized as part of employee cost.
KEYWORDS: recruitment, staffing, agency, headhunter, hiring fee, placement fee, temp agency

---
STANDARD: IAS 37
PARAGRAPH: 14
TOPIC: Insurance Premiums
RULE: Insurance premiums covering future periods are initially recorded as prepaid assets and expensed ratably over the coverage period. Journal entry on payment: Debit Prepaid Insurance (Asset), Credit Accounts Payable. Monthly: Debit Insurance Expense, Credit Prepaid Insurance.
KEYWORDS: insurance, premium, indemnity, liability insurance, professional indemnity, cyber insurance, coverage

---
STANDARD: ASC 840
PARAGRAPH: 16
TOPIC: Printing and Reproduction Services
RULE: Printing, copying, and document reproduction services used in operations are operating expenses incurred in the period. Journal entry: Debit Printing and Stationery Expense (OPEX), Credit Accounts Payable. Large print runs for marketing may be classified under Marketing Expense.
KEYWORDS: printing, reproduction, copying, brochure, flyer, banner, signage, print run

---
STANDARD: IAS 16
PARAGRAPH: 15
TOPIC: Vehicle and Fleet Expenses
RULE: Company vehicles with useful life over 1 year are capitalized as fixed assets. Journal entry: Debit Vehicles (CAPEX), Credit Accounts Payable. Running costs (fuel, servicing, insurance) are OPEX. Debit Vehicle Running Costs, Credit Accounts Payable. Leased vehicles under IFRS 16 require right-of-use asset recognition for leases over 12 months.
KEYWORDS: vehicle, car, van, fleet, automobile, truck, lease vehicle, company car

---
STANDARD: ASC 605
PARAGRAPH: 25
TOPIC: Freight Shipping and Delivery
RULE: Freight and shipping costs for delivering goods to customers are selling expenses (OPEX). Freight costs for receiving inventory are part of inventory cost (capitalized into inventory value). Journal entry for delivery: Debit Freight Out Expense, Credit Accounts Payable. For receiving: Debit Inventory, Credit Accounts Payable.
KEYWORDS: freight, shipping, delivery, courier, logistics, DHL, FedEx, UPS, postage, dispatch

---
STANDARD: IAS 16
PARAGRAPH: 7b
TOPIC: Tangible Fixed Assets - Furniture and Fittings
RULE: Furniture, fixtures, and high-value decorative items such as rugs, art, and furnishings with a cost above the capitalization threshold and useful life exceeding one accounting period are capitalized as Furniture and Fittings under Property Plant and Equipment. Journal entry: Debit Furniture and Fittings (CAPEX), Credit Accounts Payable. Low-value items below the capitalization threshold ($500-$2,500 depending on company policy) are expensed as Office Supplies Expense or Sundry Expenses.
KEYWORDS: rug, carpet, furniture, furnishings, fixtures, fittings, decorative, art, painting

STANDARD: IAS 1 / ASC 205
PARAGRAPH: IAS 1.15-17 / ASC 205-10
TOPIC: Substance Over Form and Fair Presentation
RULE: Classify transactions based on economic substance, not invoice wording alone. When invoice text conflicts with contract terms, service period, delivery evidence, asset control, or business purpose, use the underlying substance.
GL_CLASSIFICATION: Depends on substance: asset, liability, equity, revenue, expense, COGS, CAPEX, OPEX, finance cost, or tax.
JOURNAL_ENTRY: Use the entry required by the underlying transaction after inspecting contract, invoice, receipt, PO, service period, and approval trail.
AUDIT_CHECKS: Compare invoice description, PO, contract, delivery evidence, vendor master, payment terms, and period of benefit.
RED_FLAGS: vague invoice description, round-dollar invoice, related-party vendor, missing support, management override, unusual coding, invoice split below approval threshold.
KEYWORDS: substance over form, fair presentation, economic substance, classification, audit judgment, supporting documents

STANDARD: IAS 1 / ASC 250
PARAGRAPH: IAS 1.27-28 / ASC 250-10
TOPIC: Accrual Basis and Period Cut-Off
RULE: Revenue and expenses are recognized when earned or incurred, not when cash is received or paid. If goods or services were received before period-end but invoiced after period-end, an accrual is required.
GL_CLASSIFICATION: Accrued Expense, Accrued Revenue, Accounts Payable, Revenue, Expense.
JOURNAL_ENTRY: Accrue expense: Debit Expense or Asset, Credit Accrued Liabilities. Reverse on invoice receipt: Debit Accrued Liabilities, Credit Accounts Payable.
AUDIT_CHECKS: Search post-period invoices, unmatched POs, receiving reports, vendor statements, payroll cut-off, utility bills, and legal invoices.
RED_FLAGS: invoices after close for prior-period services, no accrual reversal, unusually low expenses near period-end, manual top-side accruals.
KEYWORDS: accrual, cut-off, incurred, received not invoiced, GRNI, unbilled, period end, matching

STANDARD: IAS 1 / ASC 210
PARAGRAPH: IAS 1.66-76 / ASC 210-10
TOPIC: Current vs Noncurrent Classification
RULE: Assets and liabilities expected to be realized, consumed, settled, or paid within the normal operating cycle or within 12 months are current. Amounts due beyond 12 months are noncurrent unless due on demand or covenant breach makes them current.
GL_CLASSIFICATION: Current Asset, Noncurrent Asset, Current Liability, Noncurrent Liability.
JOURNAL_ENTRY: Reclass current portion: Debit Long-Term Liability, Credit Current Portion of Debt.
AUDIT_CHECKS: Inspect maturity schedules, loan agreements, covenant compliance, payment terms, and aging reports.
RED_FLAGS: covenant breach not considered, old receivables shown current without collection support, long-term debt not split.
KEYWORDS: current, noncurrent, long term, short term, maturity, due within one year, operating cycle

STANDARD: IAS 16 / ASC 360
PARAGRAPH: IAS 16.12-14 / ASC 360-10
TOPIC: Repairs and Maintenance vs Capital Improvements
RULE: Routine repairs and maintenance are expensed as incurred. Costs that enhance capacity, extend useful life, improve performance beyond original condition, or replace a major component may be capitalized if recognition criteria are met.
GL_CLASSIFICATION: Repairs Expense, Maintenance Expense, Fixed Asset Addition, Leasehold Improvement.
JOURNAL_ENTRY: Routine repair: Debit Repairs and Maintenance Expense, Credit AP. Capital improvement: Debit Fixed Asset or Leasehold Improvement, Credit AP.
AUDIT_CHECKS: Review work orders, engineering description, useful-life impact, component replacement evidence, and before/after condition.
RED_FLAGS: normal repairs capitalized, major replacement capitalized without removing old component, expense deferral to improve EBITDA.
KEYWORDS: repair, maintenance, upgrade, overhaul, improvement, renovation, replacement, refurbishment

STANDARD: IFRS 16 / ASC 842
PARAGRAPH: IFRS 16.22 / ASC 842-20
TOPIC: Lease Recognition - Lessee Accounting
RULE: A lease generally creates a right-of-use asset and lease liability when the entity controls the use of an identified asset for a period of time in exchange for consideration. IFRS allows exemptions for short-term leases and low-value assets. US GAAP recognizes both operating and finance leases on balance sheet for lessees.
GL_CLASSIFICATION: Right-of-Use Asset, Lease Liability, Lease Expense, Depreciation, Interest Expense.
JOURNAL_ENTRY: Commencement: Debit Right-of-Use Asset, Credit Lease Liability. Payment: Debit Lease Liability and Interest Expense, Credit Cash or AP.
AUDIT_CHECKS: Inspect lease term, renewal options, discount rate, embedded leases, non-lease components, commencement date, and exemptions.
RED_FLAGS: long-term lease expensed only monthly, embedded lease missed in service contract, renewal option excluded without support.
KEYWORDS: lease, rent, right-of-use, ROU, lease liability, office lease, vehicle lease, equipment lease

STANDARD: IFRS 16 / ASC 842
PARAGRAPH: IFRS 16.5-6 / ASC 842-20-25
TOPIC: Short-Term and Low-Value Leases
RULE: Short-term leases of 12 months or less and, under IFRS, low-value asset leases may be expensed if the entity elects the exemption. Purchase options or likely renewals may prevent short-term treatment.
GL_CLASSIFICATION: Rent Expense, Lease Expense, Equipment Rental Expense.
JOURNAL_ENTRY: Debit Rent Expense or Lease Expense, Credit AP or Cash.
AUDIT_CHECKS: Verify lease term, renewal options, cancellation rights, asset value, and policy election.
RED_FLAGS: rolling 12-month contracts that are economically long-term, renewal options ignored, exemption applied inconsistently.
KEYWORDS: short-term lease, low-value lease, rental, coworking, temporary office, month to month

STANDARD: ASC 350-40 / IAS 38
PARAGRAPH: ASC 350-40-25 / IAS 38.57
TOPIC: Internal-Use Software Development Costs
RULE: Internal-use software costs are expensed during the preliminary project stage, capitalized during the application development stage when criteria are met, and expensed during training and post-implementation maintenance. Purchased software with useful life over one year may be capitalized if controlled by the entity.
GL_CLASSIFICATION: Software Asset, Intangible Asset, Software Development Expense, Training Expense.
JOURNAL_ENTRY: Capitalized stage: Debit Internal-Use Software Asset, Credit AP or Payroll Accrual. Expense stage: Debit Software Development Expense, Credit AP or Payroll Accrual.
AUDIT_CHECKS: Review project phase, time tracking, vendor SOW, go-live date, training costs, maintenance tickets, and useful life.
RED_FLAGS: preliminary planning capitalized, training capitalized, post-go-live support capitalized, unsupported developer time allocations.
KEYWORDS: software development, custom software, implementation, ERP, CRM, coding, configuration, system integration

STANDARD: ASC 350-40 / IAS 38
PARAGRAPH: ASC 350-40-25 / IAS 38.21
TOPIC: Cloud Computing Implementation Costs
RULE: SaaS access fees are usually service costs recognized over the service period. Certain cloud implementation costs may be deferred under US GAAP if they would qualify under internal-use software guidance. Under IFRS, configuration/customization is often expensed unless a controlled intangible asset is created.
GL_CLASSIFICATION: Prepaid SaaS, Deferred Implementation Cost, Implementation Expense, Subscription Expense.
JOURNAL_ENTRY: Eligible deferral: Debit Deferred Cloud Implementation Costs, Credit AP. Amortization: Debit Implementation Amortization Expense, Credit Deferred Costs.
AUDIT_CHECKS: Separate hosting access, configuration, customization, training, data conversion, maintenance, and contract term.
RED_FLAGS: all cloud implementation capitalized, training/data cleansing deferred, no SaaS contract term support.
KEYWORDS: cloud implementation, SaaS implementation, configuration, customization, migration, data conversion, setup fee

STANDARD: IFRS 15 / ASC 606
PARAGRAPH: IFRS 15.22-30 / ASC 606-10-25
TOPIC: Revenue Recognition - Five Step Model
RULE: Revenue is recognized by applying the five-step model: identify contract, identify performance obligations, determine transaction price, allocate transaction price, and recognize revenue when or as obligations are satisfied. Billing alone does not determine revenue recognition.
GL_CLASSIFICATION: Revenue, Contract Asset, Contract Liability, Deferred Revenue, Accounts Receivable.
JOURNAL_ENTRY: Invoice before performance: Debit AR, Credit Deferred Revenue. Revenue recognition: Debit Deferred Revenue or Contract Asset, Credit Revenue.
AUDIT_CHECKS: Inspect contract, PO, invoice, delivery evidence, acceptance clauses, variable consideration, and performance obligation matrix.
RED_FLAGS: revenue before delivery, side letters, unusual quarter-end sales, bill-and-hold, manual revenue entries, missing acceptance.
KEYWORDS: revenue, contract, performance obligation, transaction price, deferred revenue, contract liability, ASC 606, IFRS 15

STANDARD: IFRS 15 / ASC 606
PARAGRAPH: IFRS 15.35 / ASC 606-10-25-27
TOPIC: Revenue Over Time vs Point in Time
RULE: Revenue is recognized over time if the customer receives and consumes benefits as performed, controls the asset as created, or the asset has no alternative use and there is an enforceable right to payment. Otherwise recognize at a point in time when control transfers.
GL_CLASSIFICATION: Revenue, Contract Asset, Deferred Revenue.
JOURNAL_ENTRY: Over time: Debit Contract Asset or Deferred Revenue, Credit Revenue based on progress. Point in time: Debit AR, Credit Revenue when control transfers.
AUDIT_CHECKS: Review delivery terms, customer acceptance, milestones, progress measures, enforceable right to payment, and cancellation terms.
RED_FLAGS: milestone billing treated as revenue without performance evidence, unsupported percentage completion, point-in-time product revenue spread incorrectly.
KEYWORDS: over time, point in time, milestone, progress, control transfer, customer acceptance

STANDARD: IFRS 15 / ASC 606
PARAGRAPH: IFRS 15.106-108 / ASC 606-10-45
TOPIC: Deferred Revenue, Contract Assets, and Unbilled Revenue
RULE: Amounts billed or collected before performance are contract liabilities or deferred revenue. If revenue is recognized before the unconditional right to invoice, record contract asset or unbilled revenue and reclassify to AR when billing right becomes unconditional.
GL_CLASSIFICATION: Deferred Revenue, Contract Liability, Contract Asset, Unbilled Revenue, Revenue.
JOURNAL_ENTRY: Advance billing: Debit AR/Cash, Credit Deferred Revenue. Earned unbilled revenue: Debit Contract Asset, Credit Revenue. Billing later: Debit AR, Credit Contract Asset.
AUDIT_CHECKS: Reconcile deferred revenue rollforward, service periods, unbilled schedules, milestone evidence, and subsequent invoices.
RED_FLAGS: annual contracts recognized upfront, old unbilled balances, negative deferred revenue, revenue schedule missing.
KEYWORDS: deferred revenue, contract asset, unbilled revenue, unearned revenue, advance billing, contract liability

STANDARD: IFRS 15 / ASC 606
PARAGRAPH: IFRS 15.50-59 / ASC 606-10-32
TOPIC: Variable Consideration and Refund Liabilities
RULE: Discounts, rebates, credits, penalties, returns, SLA credits, and performance bonuses are variable consideration. Estimate variable consideration and constrain revenue to amounts not expected to reverse significantly.
GL_CLASSIFICATION: Revenue Reduction, Refund Liability, Accrued Rebate, Contract Liability.
JOURNAL_ENTRY: Estimate rebate or refund: Debit Revenue or Sales Discounts, Credit Refund Liability or Accrued Rebates.
AUDIT_CHECKS: Review historical returns, rebate agreements, SLA clauses, credit notes, and post-period adjustments.
RED_FLAGS: rebates ignored, no constraint analysis, large credit notes after year-end, manual revenue top-side entries.
KEYWORDS: rebate, discount, refund, return, credit note, variable consideration, SLA credit, penalty

STANDARD: IFRS 15 / ASC 606
PARAGRAPH: IFRS 15.B34-B38 / ASC 606-10-55
TOPIC: Principal vs Agent Revenue
RULE: If the entity controls the good or service before transfer to the customer, it is principal and recognizes gross revenue. If it arranges for another party to provide the good or service, it is agent and recognizes net commission or fee revenue.
GL_CLASSIFICATION: Gross Revenue, Net Revenue, Commission Revenue, COGS.
JOURNAL_ENTRY: Principal: Debit AR, Credit Revenue; Debit COGS, Credit Inventory/AP. Agent: Debit AR/Cash, Credit Commission Revenue and supplier payable as applicable.
AUDIT_CHECKS: Review control indicators, inventory risk, pricing discretion, fulfillment responsibility, and supplier/customer contracts.
RED_FLAGS: gross revenue for pass-through services, no COGS for principal arrangement, inconsistent treatment by contract.
KEYWORDS: principal, agent, gross revenue, net revenue, marketplace, reseller, pass through, commission

STANDARD: IFRS 15 / ASC 606
PARAGRAPH: IFRS 15.B79-B82 / ASC 606-10-55
TOPIC: Bill-and-Hold Arrangements
RULE: Bill-and-hold revenue is recognized only when strict criteria are met, including substantive reason, product separately identified as customer-owned, ready for transfer, and inability to use or redirect the product.
GL_CLASSIFICATION: Revenue, Deferred Revenue, Inventory, COGS.
JOURNAL_ENTRY: If criteria met: Debit AR, Credit Revenue; Debit COGS, Credit Inventory. If not met: Debit AR/Cash, Credit Deferred Revenue.
AUDIT_CHECKS: Inspect customer request, segregation evidence, readiness for delivery, shipping schedule, and inability to redirect.
RED_FLAGS: quarter-end bill-and-hold without customer request, goods not segregated, goods still available for other customers.
KEYWORDS: bill and hold, warehouse, customer request, delivery delay, revenue cut-off, segregation

STANDARD: IFRS 15 / ASC 606
PARAGRAPH: IFRS 15.B20-B27 / ASC 606-10-55
TOPIC: Consignment Inventory
RULE: Products delivered on consignment remain inventory of the consignor until control transfers to an end customer or other sale criteria are met. Delivery to the consignee alone does not create revenue.
GL_CLASSIFICATION: Consignment Inventory, Revenue, COGS.
JOURNAL_ENTRY: Transfer to consignee: reclassify inventory if needed, no revenue. End sale: Debit AR/Cash, Credit Revenue; Debit COGS, Credit Inventory.
AUDIT_CHECKS: Review consignment agreements, sell-through reports, return rights, inventory confirmations, and cut-off.
RED_FLAGS: revenue recorded when shipped to consignee, consigned inventory omitted from count, old consigned stock not reserved.
KEYWORDS: consignment, consignee, sell-through, channel inventory, return rights, distributor

STANDARD: IFRS 15 / ASC 606
PARAGRAPH: IFRS 15.B64-B76 / ASC 606-10-55
TOPIC: Customer Options, Loyalty Points, and Material Rights
RULE: Loyalty points, renewal discounts, coupons, free goods, and customer options are separate performance obligations if they provide a material right beyond what similar customers receive. Allocate transaction price and defer revenue for the material right.
GL_CLASSIFICATION: Deferred Revenue, Contract Liability, Revenue.
JOURNAL_ENTRY: Debit Cash/AR, Credit Revenue and Deferred Revenue based on allocation. On redemption or expiry: Debit Deferred Revenue, Credit Revenue.
AUDIT_CHECKS: Review loyalty terms, redemption history, standalone selling prices, breakage estimates, and expiry.
RED_FLAGS: loyalty points ignored, all revenue recognized upfront, unsupported breakage assumption.
KEYWORDS: loyalty points, coupon, material right, renewal option, discount, free goods, rewards

STANDARD: IFRS 15 / ASC 606
PARAGRAPH: IFRS 15.60-65 / ASC 606-10-32
TOPIC: Significant Financing Component
RULE: If payment timing provides a significant financing benefit to customer or entity, adjust transaction price for time value of money unless a practical expedient applies, such as one year or less between transfer and payment.
GL_CLASSIFICATION: Revenue, Interest Income, Interest Expense, Contract Asset, Contract Liability.
JOURNAL_ENTRY: Seller financing: recognize revenue at present value and interest income over time.
AUDIT_CHECKS: Compare payment timing to performance timing, interest rates, advance payments, and financing purpose.
RED_FLAGS: multi-year upfront payments without financing assessment, extended payment terms treated as normal revenue.
KEYWORDS: significant financing, advance payment, extended terms, time value, interest income, deferred payment

STANDARD: ASC 340-40 / IFRS 15
PARAGRAPH: ASC 340-40-25 / IFRS 15.91-98
TOPIC: Contract Costs - Sales Commissions and Fulfillment Costs
RULE: Incremental costs of obtaining a contract, such as recoverable sales commissions, are capitalized unless the amortization period is one year or less and practical expedient is elected. Fulfillment costs are capitalized only if they relate directly to a contract, generate resources used to satisfy obligations, and are expected to be recovered.
GL_CLASSIFICATION: Deferred Contract Cost, Commission Expense, Contract Fulfillment Asset, COGS.
JOURNAL_ENTRY: Capitalize commission: Debit Deferred Contract Costs, Credit Commissions Payable. Amortize: Debit Commission Expense, Credit Deferred Contract Costs.
AUDIT_CHECKS: Review commission plan, contract term, renewal commissions, clawbacks, direct cost link, and recoverability.
RED_FLAGS: commissions capitalized for failed bids, all commissions expensed despite multi-year contracts, general overhead capitalized.
KEYWORDS: sales commission, contract cost, deferred commission, fulfillment cost, onboarding, customer acquisition cost

STANDARD: IAS 2 / ASC 330
PARAGRAPH: IAS 2.10-18 / ASC 330-10
TOPIC: Inventory Cost Components
RULE: Inventory cost includes purchase price, import duties, non-refundable taxes, freight-in, handling, conversion costs, and costs directly attributable to bringing inventory to present location and condition. Inventory is expensed as COGS when sold.
GL_CLASSIFICATION: Inventory Asset, Raw Materials, Work in Process, Finished Goods, COGS.
JOURNAL_ENTRY: Purchase: Debit Inventory, Credit AP. Sale: Debit COGS, Credit Inventory.
AUDIT_CHECKS: Verify receiving, ownership transfer, freight terms, costing method, physical count, and cut-off.
RED_FLAGS: freight-in expensed while inventory remains unsold, obsolete stock not reserved, missing receiving documents.
KEYWORDS: inventory, stock, raw materials, finished goods, landed cost, freight-in, COGS

STANDARD: IAS 2 / ASC 330
PARAGRAPH: IAS 2.28-33 / ASC 330-10-35
TOPIC: Inventory Net Realizable Value and Obsolescence
RULE: Inventory is written down when cost is not recoverable due to damage, obsolescence, slow movement, declining selling prices, or excess stock. IFRS uses lower of cost and net realizable value; US GAAP treatment depends on inventory method.
GL_CLASSIFICATION: Inventory Write-Down Expense, Inventory Reserve, COGS.
JOURNAL_ENTRY: Debit Inventory Write-Down Expense or COGS, Credit Inventory or Inventory Reserve.
AUDIT_CHECKS: Analyze aging, slow-moving items, post-period sales, margins, physical damage, and discontinued products.
RED_FLAGS: old stock with no reserve, negative margins, large discounts after year-end, management override of reserve.
KEYWORDS: obsolete inventory, NRV, write-down, slow moving, damaged goods, excess stock, reserve

STANDARD: IAS 2 / ASC 330
PARAGRAPH: IAS 2.16 / ASC 330-10
TOPIC: Abnormal Waste, Storage, and Production Inefficiency
RULE: Abnormal waste, excessive spoilage, avoidable rework, idle capacity costs, and storage not necessary for production are expensed as incurred rather than included in inventory.
GL_CLASSIFICATION: Production Variance Expense, Scrap Expense, Storage Expense.
JOURNAL_ENTRY: Debit Scrap or Production Variance Expense, Credit Inventory or AP.
AUDIT_CHECKS: Compare standard vs actual usage, scrap reports, capacity utilization, and warehouse purpose.
RED_FLAGS: abnormal losses capitalized into inventory, unexplained production variances, no scrap approval.
KEYWORDS: scrap, spoilage, abnormal waste, rework, storage, idle capacity, production variance

STANDARD: IAS 2 / ASC 330
